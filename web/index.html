Triage

Only the email that needs you.

Give Triage access to your email inbox and allow it to manage junk and send you notifications on Telegram.

Ask to join How it works

Your mail stays yours. It is encrypted under your own key, so Helen never sees it. Triage never keeps the body of an email, and you can leave at any time and take everything with you.

It starts by only telling you. It touches nothing in your mailbox until you say so.

Helen built Triage for her own four inboxes, and now shares it with a few people she knows.

01What it does

It sorts every new email into one of four piles, and only one of them interrupts you.

🔴 Needs youA real person waiting on you, money that needs a decision, a security problem, a deadline. You hear straight away.
📬 For informationReceipts, deliveries, confirmations. Gathered into a digest at 8am and 5pm, UK time.
📰 NewslettersAsked about once per sender: Unsubscribe or Keep. Unsubscribing is one tap.
🗑 JunkSpam and phishing are counted, never answered, and never clicked.

It starts in watch mode: it sorts and tells you, but never touches your mailbox unless you tap a button. Switch to act mode with /mode act and it also archives newsletters and junk as they arrive.

02How to join

About two minutes. You need Telegram and a Gmail or Google Workspace inbox.

  1. Tap Ask to join. It opens the Triage bot in Telegram. Press Start.
  2. Wait for a yes. Helen approves each person herself. When she does, the bot says hello.
  3. Connect your inbox. Send /connect Gmail and sign in with Google. Google will warn that it "hasn't verified this app", because Triage is a small private tool rather than a public product. Tap Advanced, then Go to triage.hw-tools.io, then allow access.
  4. That is it. From then on, only what needs you arrives. /status shows what is connected and /help lists everything.

03Your privacy

Your mail is yours. Helen runs Triage, and it is built so that she never sees it.

  • It never stores the body of an email. For 30 days it keeps who an email was from, the subject and a one-line summary, so the digest works. Then they are deleted.
  • All of that is encrypted under a key of your own before it is stored, along with your inbox address and your sender choices. Browsing the database shows nothing readable, including to Helen.
  • Helen sees only that you joined, your Telegram name when you ask, and how many inboxes you connected. Never which ones, and nothing about your mail.
  • Your email is sorted by Anthropic's Claude. The text of each new email is sent to Anthropic for that alone. Anthropic does not train on it.
  • Alerts arrive in your own private Telegram chat with the bot. Nobody else is in it. Like any Telegram chat, it is stored on Telegram's servers.
  • You can leave at any time. Send /leave and every inbox is disconnected, Google's access is handed back and everything held for you is deleted. You can also withdraw access yourself at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

In fairness: Helen owns the service, so your privacy rests on trust as well as on encryption. Someone who controls the code could change it. Triage is built so that she never sees your mail, and she has promised not to look.

Use of data received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising. The full terms are on the Legal page.

04Good to know
  • Gmail and Google Workspace only, for now. Not iCloud or Outlook.
  • A work account may be blocked by your organisation's own Google administrator. Personal Gmail always works.
  • It only looks at mail that arrives after you connect. It never trawls your old email.
  • It is free and small on purpose: a few people, each approved by Helen.
  • It never opens links from your email or shows them as buttons, except the unsubscribe page of a sender you have just chosen to leave.
